The model includes gravity, but it does not solve gas pressure, heat, or deformation. Unlike DarcyModel, it has no additional independent saturated compressibility storage. If the chosen retention law has a perfectly flat saturated branch, that branch's storage derivative vanishes. A smooth retention extension such as ExponentialCutoff changes this behavior; it must be treated as a modeling choice, not assumed equivalent to an arbitrary saturated storativity.

The saturation-weighted pressure above omits that contribution; it is not the full energetic expression (Dangla, undated). Furthermore, a single-valued retention curve does not describe wetting–drying hysteresis: that requires information about the loading history.
